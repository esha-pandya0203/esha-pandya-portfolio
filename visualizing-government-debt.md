For this graph, I knew I wanted to highlight some kind of trend over time, so I started by limiting the x-axis to the years 2007 and 2024. I chose this time period because it held the most data for the most countries, with few blank values in between. Keeping all of the countries in the chart easily became cluttered, so I created a subset that contained a mix of high-debt, low-debt, increasing, and decreasing countries. Using a slope chart, I could easily visualize whether the country's debt-GDP ratio increased or decreased since 2007. Noticing that only four countries were able to decrease their debt-GDP ratio, I chose to highlight those countries: Ireland, Germany, Sweden, and Switzerland. All other countries either remained around the same point as when they started out in 2007 or significantly increased since then. I labeled and used color for only the four countries with decreasing ratios to avoid clutter, allowing the reader to easily identify the main takeaway.
